Detailed Description

template<Range R>
struct daw::pipelines::take_view< R >

Holds the source range and the requested count only. The sized iterators are built in begin( ), so they always refer to this object's current location and the view can be safely copied and moved, like map_view and filter_view. Caching them at construction would leave their parent pointers dangling after a copy/move.
